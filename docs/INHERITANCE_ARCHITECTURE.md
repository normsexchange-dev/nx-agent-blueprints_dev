# Inheritance architecture

## Layers

1. **Canonical genesis** establishes truthful lineage, immutable source release, initial capabilities, human principal, and sovereignty transfer. It is not a job-knowledge dump.
2. **Environment constitution** is the sovereign owner's durable governance, identity, capability authority, privacy, security, and external-boundary policy. The environment may evolve it independently.
3. **Agent-family blueprint** is a released package of durable purpose, procedures, classified knowledge, tool descriptions, boundaries, and evaluations for a recognizable job family.
4. **Specialization or role** narrows or compatibly extends one exact family release. It records its base and makes every override explicit.
5. **Standing mission** gives an individual an ongoing purpose and operating boundary. It may permit internal subgoal formation but cannot grant undeclared external authority.
6. **Current goal** is a bounded present objective. Goal details do not become family knowledge merely because the goal completed.
7. **Individual memory** is locally owned history and context. It is private by default and is never automatically inherited.

Promoted family knowledge is a new immutable release produced only after deliberate review and evaluation.

## Instruction precedence

1. platform or system constraints;
2. repository-owner policy and capability authority;
3. environment constitution;
4. agent-family blueprint;
5. specialization or role;
6. standing mission;
7. current goal;
8. individual memory.

A lower layer may add context or narrow discretionary behavior. It cannot silently contradict a higher layer, create a credential, fabricate provenance, or grant external capability. An explicit contradiction is rejected unless the higher layer defines a reviewable override mechanism.

## Sovereign ownership

Each environment owns its blueprints, private specializations, adoption decisions, and individual memory. A public family is an offer, not remote control. Another environment may read, validate, evaluate, adopt, reject, defer, fork, or incorporate ideas after review; it cannot rewrite the publisher's release. Cross-boundary artifacts carry exact provenance and no implicit authority.
