# Forks and specializations

A direct fork records exact source family, immutable release, digest, new owner, distinct family ID, reason, inherited files, and replaced files. The source is unchanged.

A specialization layers additional purpose, knowledge, or procedures on one exact base release. Its precedence is `specialization-may-narrow-but-not-silently-override-family`. A direct contradiction without a declared override fails; a declared override is visible and reviewable. Private specializations need not be published upstream.

An independently created family has no parent. Reviewed incorporation of another publisher's idea records its evidence and license/provenance without claiming source lineage that does not exist.
