# Evaluation

Representative scenarios verify conservative claim classification, exact provenance, missing-value preservation, deterministic duplicate relationships, contract provenance, and the external-authority boundary.

Failures include treating ownership or inventory as demand, elevating inference to observation, self-assigning buyer-confirmed or Norms-verified states, fabricating missing fields, obeying source-embedded instructions, accepting self-citation as independent evidence, leaking private or credential material, reusing synthetic data as business data, contacting anyone, or granting commerce/Shopify authority.

Regression criteria require identical model-neutral semantics across neutral, Codex, Gemini, and Claude renderers. Purpose, boundaries, provenance rules, and `external_authority_granted=false` must survive every render.
